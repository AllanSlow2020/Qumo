The shape

Three doors, one accrual path

A shopper reaches Qumo three ways, and only two of them can award anything. The distinction matters more than it looks: a poster is one static code that anyone walking past can scan, so it proves no purchase and must never carry value. A slip or sticker is unique per transaction, which is what makes earning safe.

Both earning doors converge on applyAccrual() — one function, one ledger write, one place where caps and stamp-card completion live. Adding SMS adds an adapter, not a second set of rules.

Earning

What a slip has to survive

Nine checks between a scanned slip and a ledger row. Seven exist. The two that don't are the reason unsigned stores can't ship yet — without a per-person cap, a shopper at a store whose POS can't sign controls every field of the URL and the single-use constraint only stops them reusing a transaction number, not choosing a fresh one.

Deployment

Where it all runs

One engine, three adapters, one database — and no runtime connection to CIOS at all. That last part is the change: the earlier plan had Qumo sharing CIOS's database so a shopper's WhatsApp check-ins and their scans could land in one balance. Brand-scoped loyalty removes the reason, and sharing was expensive — seven of Qumo's eight tables held foreign keys into CIOS-owned ones.

Sequence

Order of work

Lettered because it is genuinely a sequence — each phase depends on the one before it. The security work sits before extraction deliberately: those fixes travel with the engine, and fixing them once beats fixing them in two repos.

  1. Decide and documentThis page, plus the architecture note, committed to the repo. No code.
  2. Engine hardeningThe two missing gates from Fig 3. Opt-out. Data export. Slip path first, since Chicken Licken is the pilot.
  3. ExtractionNew repo, new database. Copy the engine, drop everything CIOS-shaped.
  4. Brand identity layerSubdomain resolution, theme fields, and inverting the shell so the brand leads and Qumo is the footer.
  5. Brand consoleOwn app, own auth, own navigation. Promotions, codes, stores, analytics.
  6. Subscription lifecycleBilling, and the freeze-earning-honour-redemption rule enforced in the engine rather than the UI.
  7. SMS channelBalance lookup and SMS-to-earn over a rented short code. USSD only if the pilot shows demand.

Unresolved

Blocking, and not yet answered

The domain is load-bearing now

qumo.app resolves to an address held by someone else, and so does qumo.com. When it was one wallet domain that was cosmetic. With a subdomain per brand it is what goes on a Chicken Licken poster — and a poster cannot carry a hostname we do not control. This blocks Phase D, not Phase B.

Operator or responsible party

Brand-specific sites with per-brand opt-in make "the brand is responsible party, Qumo is the operator" the natural reading — but that is a lawyer's confirmation, and it has to land before the first real shopper registers. Retrofitting consent onto people who already signed up is materially harder than getting it right once.

An aggregator account

The same account serves both the login passcodes Phase B already depends on and the Phase G balance channel. Worth pricing reverse-billed against standard-rated early: that number sets the per-brand monthly cap, and this is the first feature whose cost scales with engagement rather than staying flat.

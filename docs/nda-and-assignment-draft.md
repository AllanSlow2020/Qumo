# Two short documents, drafted for review

1 November 2026 · drafted for review, not for use

Both are short, both are standard in shape, and both are here so a lawyer
redlines rather than drafts. Neither is legal advice. Flags as elsewhere:
`[[QUERY]]` for a lawyer to settle, `[[DOUBLE BRACKETS]]` for a fact to

The assignment is the one to do first. It costs almost nothing now and
gets awkward later, because the longer the company trades on work it may
not own, the more parties have an interest in the answer.

# A. Assignment of intellectual property

**Why this exists.** QUMO-APP (PTY) LTD and Allan Slow are different legal
persons. The work was done by Allan personally, which means the copyright
may sit with him rather than with the company that trades on it. Harmless
until a raise, a sale or a disagreement, and then not harmless at all.

**Parties**

- **The Assignor:** Allan Slow, identity number `[[ID NUMBER]]`,
  `[[ADDRESS]]`.
- **The Company:** QUMO-APP (PTY) LTD, registration 2026/858757/07.
  `[[REGISTERED ADDRESS]]`

**1. What is assigned**

All of the Assignor's right, title and interest in the intellectual
property created by or for him in connection with Qumo, whenever created,
including:

1. the software, in source and compiled form, comprising the Qumo
   platform, its console, its shopper surface and its supporting tools;
2. the written material: documentation, briefs, legal copy drafts, pitch
   and product decks, and site copy;
3. the designs, marks, logos and visual identity used under the name Qumo;
4. the name **Qumo** itself and any goodwill in it, together with the
   right to apply for and hold registered trade marks in it;
5. the domain names `qumo.co.za` and `qumo-app.com`;
6. all related rights of action, including the right to sue for
   infringements occurring before this assignment.

**2. When it takes effect**

On signature, and retrospectively in respect of everything already
created. `[[QUERY: whether a present assignment of future works is
effective here, or whether this needs a standing obligation to assign as

**3. Consideration**

`[[QUERY: what, if anything. The parties are not at arm's length and the
Assignor is the Company's `[[sole?]]` director and shareholder. A lawyer
should say whether nominal consideration is needed and what the tax
treatment is.]]`

**4. Further assurance**

The Assignor will sign whatever else is reasonably needed to record the
Company as owner, including with CIPC, a trade mark registry or a domain
registrar.

**5. Work created with AI tools**

Most of the platform was written by Claude, an Anthropic model, directed
by the Assignor: 97 of the repository's commits are attributed to the
model and 38 to a person. The provider's terms assign ownership of the
output to the customer, and South African copyright law addresses
computer-generated works directly, attributing authorship to the person
who made the arrangements necessary for the work's creation.

This clause assigns whatever the Assignor holds in that output, without
admitting that any gap exists.

# B. Mutual non-disclosure agreement

**Why this exists.** To send before a substantive brand conversation. Two
things worth knowing before relying on it:

- **Large brands and their agencies often will not sign one** from a small
  supplier. Plan for the conversation happening without it.
- **It does not protect the idea.** Nothing does. It protects the detail:
  figures, roadmap, the workings of the engine, and anything said about
  other brands.

The pitch deck is written to survive travelling. What is genuinely
defensible, the engine, the ledger and the proof model, is not in it.

**Parties**

QUMO-APP (PTY) LTD, registration 2026/858757/07, and `[[COUNTERPARTY]]`,
registration `[[NUMBER]]`. Mutual: each may disclose and each may receive.

**1. Purpose**

Evaluating whether the parties will work together on a loyalty programme.
Confidential information may be used for that and nothing else.

**2. What is confidential**

Information disclosed by either party that is marked confidential, or that
a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or
the circumstances of its disclosure. For Qumo this expressly includes
pricing, the product roadmap, the workings of the platform, and anything
said about another brand. `[[QUERY: whether to require marking. Requiring
it is cleaner and routinely forgotten in a live meeting.]]`

**3. What is not**

Information that is public without breach of this agreement, was already
lawfully held by the recipient, is independently developed without use of
the disclosure, or is lawfully received from a third party.

**4. Obligations**

Keep it confidential, use it only for the purpose, disclose it internally
only to those who need it for the purpose and are under equivalent duties,
and protect it with at least reasonable care.

**5. Compelled disclosure**

Permitted where required by law or a regulator, with notice to the other
party where lawful, and limited to what is required.

**6. Term**

`[[QUERY: how long. A common shape is two years from disclosure, with
anything that is a trade secret protected for as long as it stays one.]]`

**7. No licence, no obligation**

Nothing here grants any right in the other party's intellectual property,
and nothing obliges either party to proceed.

**8. Return or destruction**

On request, the recipient returns or destroys the confidential
information, subject to backups and to any legal retention duty.

signature.]]` South African law assumed.
